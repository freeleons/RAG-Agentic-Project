# ApexCare HR Supplemental Policies & Guidelines (2026)

*This document serves as the authoritative knowledge base for ApexCare policies not covered by standard carrier plan summaries. This is optimized for LLM/RAG retrieval.*

---

## 1. WEX Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) Financial Limits
*Note: For instructions on how to use the WEX mobile app or debit card, refer to the WEX Benefits Technology Guide. The following outlines the financial limits for the 2026 plan year.*

*   **Healthcare FSA Maximum Annual Contribution:** $3,200 per employee.
*   **Healthcare FSA Rollover Limit:** Up to $640 of unused funds from the current plan year may be rolled over into the 2026 plan year. Any unused funds exceeding $640 will be forfeited (Use-It-Or-Lose-It rule).
*   **Dependent Care FSA Maximum Annual Contribution:** $5,000 per household ($2,500 if married and filing separate tax returns).
*   **Dependent Care FSA Rollover:** Dependent Care FSAs do *not* qualify for rollover.
*   **Run-Out Period:** Employees have until March 31st of the following calendar year to submit receipts and claims for expenses incurred during the active plan year.

## 2. Work From Anywhere (WFA) & Remote Work Policy
ApexCare supports a flexible work environment to promote work-life balance while ensuring operational excellence.

*   **Eligibility:** All full-time employees are eligible for the WFA program after completing their initial 90-day introductory period.
*   **WFA Travel Allowance:** Employees may work from any location within the United States for up to 60 calendar days per year without impacting their primary tax status.
*   **International WFA:** Working outside of the United States requires pre-approval from both HR and the Information Security team due to data compliance, and is strictly limited to 14 consecutive days.
*   **Core Working Hours:** Regardless of physical location or time zone, all remote employees must be online and available during ApexCare’s core collaboration hours: **10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST**.
*   **Home Office Stipend:** Employees classified as "Fully Remote" receive a one-time $500 home office setup stipend, plus a $50/month recurring allowance to offset personal internet and cell phone costs.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Company Holidays
ApexCare utilizes a tiered accrual system based on tenure.

*   **Accrual Rates:**
    *   **0-2 Years of Service:** 15 days (120 hours) per year.
    *   **3-5 Years of Service:** 20 days (160 hours) per year.
    *   **5+ Years of Service:** 25 days (200 hours) per year.
*   **PTO Rollover:** Employees may carry over a maximum of 40 hours of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Excess hours are forfeited on December 31st.
*   **Company Holidays:** ApexCare observes 11 paid standard federal holidays.
*   **Floating Holidays:** Employees receive 2 floating holidays per year, available on January 1st, to be used for cultural, religious, or personal events. Floating holidays do not roll over.

## 4. Paid Parental Leave
ApexCare provides comprehensive support for growing families.

*   **Primary Caregiver Leave:** Up to 16 weeks of 100% paid leave.
*   **Secondary Caregiver Leave:** Up to 8 weeks of 100% paid leave.
*   **Eligibility conditions:**
    *   Applies to birth, adoption, or placement of a foster child.
    *   Leave can be taken continuously or intermittently.
    *   Must be utilized within 12 months of the child’s birth or placement.
    *   Employees must have at least 6 months of continuous service to qualify for the full 100% paid benefit.

## 5. 401(k) Retirement Plan & Employer Match
*   **Eligibility:** Employees are eligible to participate and contribute on Day 1 of employment.
*   **Employer Match Formula:** ApexCare matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation contributed by the employee, and 50% on the next 2%. (Maximum employer match is 5% if the employee contributes 6% or more).
*   **Vesting Schedule:** All Employer Match contributions are subject to an immediate 100% vesting schedule.
*   **Auto-Enrollment:** New hires are automatically enrolled at a 4% pre-tax contribution rate unless they actively opt-out or change their election within the first 30 days.

## 6. Bereavement Leave
*   **Immediate Family:** Up to 5 days of paid leave for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, or parent-in-law).
*   **Extended Family:** Up to 3 days of paid leave for the loss of an extended family member (grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle).
*   **Travel Extension:** If significant travel is required (over 300 miles), an additional 2 days of unpaid leave may be requested and automatically approved.

## 7. Tuition Reimbursement & Professional Development
*   **Degree Programs:** ApexCare reimburses up to $5,250 per calendar year for tuition and textbooks related to a degree program that aligns with the employee's career trajectory. Reimbursement is contingent upon receiving a grade of "B" or higher.
*   **Certifications & Conferences:** Employees receive an annual professional development budget of $1,500 to be used for industry certifications, licenses, workshops, or professional conferences.
*   **Clawback Provision:** If an employee voluntarily resigns within 12 months of receiving a tuition reimbursement payout, they must repay 100% of the reimbursed amount.
